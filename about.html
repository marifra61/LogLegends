About LogLegends

The simplest way for new drivers to track supervised practice hours and stay on the road to getting licensed.

Why We Built LogLegends

Getting a driver's license is a milestone, but tracking the required practice hours shouldn't be a headache. Most families end up using paper logs, spreadsheets, or trying to remember hours from memory. That's where mistakes happen and frustration sets in.

LogLegends was built by a parent who went through the process and realized there had to be a better way. A simple app that tracks driving hours in real time, separates day and night driving, and generates the documentation you need, all from your phone.

Built for North Carolina, Works Everywhere

LogLegends is designed around North Carolina's graduated licensing requirements: 60 total supervised hours with at least 10 hours of night driving. But every state has different requirements, so the app lets you customize hour goals to match your state's rules.

Who It's For

LogLegends is designed for teen drivers working toward their license and the parents or guardians supervising them. Whether your teen just got their learner's permit or is finishing up their final hours, LogLegends makes it easy to stay organized and on track.

Driving instructors can also use LogLegends to log sessions with students and provide documented proof of practice hours.

Our Mission

We believe every new driver deserves a safe, stress-free path to getting licensed. By making it effortless to track and document practice hours, we help families focus on what matters most: building confident, safe drivers.
