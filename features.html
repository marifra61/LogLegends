Powerful Features

Everything you need to track supervised driving hours, from your first drive to your license appointment.

🗺️

Live GPS Route Tracking

Automatically record your exact route during every drive. See where you've been on an interactive map and build a verifiable record of your driving practice with real GPS data.

🌙

Automatic Night Hour Detection

LogLegends detects when you're driving at night based on sunset and sunrise times for your location. Night hours are tracked separately so you always know where you stand with your state's requirements.

📄

PDF Report Export

Generate a professional driving log report with one tap. Includes dates, times, durations, and day vs. night breakdown, ready to bring to the DMV or share with your driving instructor.

✅

Pre-Drive Safety Checklist

Build safe habits from day one. Before every drive, LogLegends prompts a quick safety check for mirrors, seatbelts, and phone, so new drivers start each session focused and prepared.

📊

Real-Time Progress Dashboard

See your total hours, night hours, and weekly progress at a glance. Visual progress indicators show exactly how close you are to meeting your state's requirements.

⚙️

Customizable State Requirements

Defaults to North Carolina's graduated licensing requirements (60 total hours, 10 night hours), but you can adjust the hour goals to match any state's rules.

📱

Works on Any Device

LogLegends is a Progressive Web App. No app store download required. Open it in your browser on any phone, tablet, or computer. Add it to your home screen for instant access.

☁️

Cloud Sync Premium

Keep your driving data safe and synced across all your devices. Parents and teens can view the same driving log from different phones. Never lose your progress.
